Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 2026

HotSeat now supports deleting your account directly from the app. Follow the steps below to permanently delete your account and associated personal data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your account, profile, and personal data will be deleted. Proceed only if you are certain you want to delete your HotSeat account.

How to Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open HotSeat and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Navigate to your Profile screen. Tap your profile avatar or the profile icon in the app's bottom navigation bar.
  3. Locate and tap Delete Account. This option appears in your profile settings.
  4. Read the confirmation dialog carefully. The dialog explains what will be deleted and that the action cannot be reversed.
  5. Confirm the deletion by tapping the confirmation button in the dialog if you want to proceed.
  6. Wait for the deletion process to complete. Once finished, you will be signed out and returned to the login/signup screen.

What Gets Deleted

When you confirm account deletion, the following data associated with your account is permanently removed:

  • Your account credentials (email, hashed password)
  • Your profile information (username, display name, avatar)

What May Be Retained

Certain information may not be deleted immediately or at all:

  • Shared league data: Fantasy league information from Sleeper (rosters, standings, matchups, transactions) is sourced from Sleeper's public API and is not your personal data. This data remains available to other league members and in Sleeper's systems.
  • Published league writeups: If you published AI-generated league recaps or writeups that are visible to other league members, those publications may remain accessible to the league.
  • Cached league data: Temporary league data cached for performance is cleared on a short cycle (a few days) independent of account deletion.
  • Backups: Database backups are typically retained for 30–90 days before being overwritten, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Legal obligations: We may retain limited records when required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

If Deletion Fails

If the Delete Account action does not complete (for example, due to a network error or server issue):

  1. Check your internet connection and try again.
  2. Force-close and reopen the app, then repeat the steps above.
  3. If the problem persists, contact us at logschindler@gmail.com with the email address associated with your HotSeat account. We will assist you with the deletion request.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal information, contact us at logschindler@gmail.com .
