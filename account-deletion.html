Request Account Deletion

```

If you would like to delete your HotSeat account and associated personal information, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to logschindler@gmail.com .
  2. In the email, provide the email address associated with the HotSeat account you want deleted.

Important: Please make sure you provide the email address associated with the HotSeat account so that we can identify the correct account.

Account deletion requests will be processed in accordance with the HotSeat Privacy Policy.

What Happens After You Request Deletion?

We will review your request and take the necessary steps to delete your HotSeat account and associated personal information. Certain information may be retained when required for legal, security, or other legitimate purposes as described in our Privacy Policy.

Future In-App Account Deletion

HotSeat is planned to support account deletion directly within the mobile app. Once this functionality is available, these instructions will be updated to explain how to initiate account deletion from within the app.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal information, contact us at logschindler@gmail.com .
